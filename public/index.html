What a frame-by-frame breakdown looks like

Same style of tips you get after uploading your own clip — timestamp, screenshot zone, concrete edit action.

0:00
0:00 · hook · high
Weak opening frame
First second is a static mid-shot with no on-screen promise. Cut the greeting, start on the strongest face/action, and put 3–5 bold words on screen by 0:00.5 so the clip works with sound off.
Move payoff to frame 1
0:07
0:07 · pacing · high
Dead stretch — low motion
Same angle holds ~4s with no cut or text change. Add a jump cut, 10% punch-in, or a short text hit on the key word so the eye gets a new beat before the swipe reflex kicks in.
Pattern interrupt every 2–3s
0:12
0:12 · captions · mid
Captions fight the face
Long line covers the mouth and sits too low (UI zone). Split into 3–5 word chunks, high contrast, center/upper third, synced to the spoken stress — not a full sentence dumped at once.
Safe zone + shorter lines
0:18
0:18 · cta · high
No clear CTA after payoff
Clip ends on the last tip with no ask. After the main point, hold 1–2s with spoken + on-screen CTA (“save this” / “part 2”) — not “like and subscribe” in the first second.
